# RelayPay – Front-End Visual Brand Asset

This document guides the appearance of the customer-facing front-end interface for the Customer Service Agent.

## Brand Overview

RelayPay is a B2B fintech product used by founders, finance leads, and operations teams to manage cross-border payments and invoicing. The product deals directly with money, compliance, and trust-sensitive workflows.

The interface should feel:
- Professional
- Calm
- Minimal
- Trustworthy

The UI should avoid anything playful, flashy, or experimental.

---

## Logo

- Place at the top-left of the interface, or centered at the top for single-screen flows
- Do not animate or stylise beyond basic sizing and spacing

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Primary Brand Color | Deep Blue |
| Secondary / Accent Color | Teal Blue |
| Background | Off-White / Light Grey |

- Buttons, active states, and highlights should use the primary or accent color sparingly

---

## Typography

- Use a clean, modern, sans-serif font
- Preferred: **Inter**, or the system UI font stack (default OS fonts)
- Avoid decorative fonts, monospace fonts, or large variations in font weight

---

## What to Avoid

- Bright or neon colors
- Gradients
- Emojis
- Chat-bubble heavy designs
- Experimental layouts
- Over-branding

If unsure, default to simpler and more neutral.
